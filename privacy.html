Fractaline — Privacy Policy

Zero Data Collected • Zero Ads • 100% Offline

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Fractaline: Infinite Coloring is designed to be a calm, private creative space. We respect your privacy completely.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information. Fractaline works 100% offline. Your saved artworks, gallery thumbnails, audio preferences, and theme settings remain strictly on your device in Apple's standard local storage and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. No Third-Party Ads, Analytics, or Tracking

Fractaline contains zero advertisements, zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero behavioral tracking, and zero account sign-ups.

3. In-App Purchases

Fractaline offers optional one-time in-app purchases to unlock additional coloring studios, brushes, and export formats. All transactions and Family Sharing entitlements are processed solely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

4. Exports & Sharing

When you export an artwork as a PNG, PDF, or timelapse video, the file is generated locally on your device and shared only with destinations you explicitly choose through iOS's standard Share sheet.

5. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Visit our Support Page or email nathan.leniz@gmail.com.
