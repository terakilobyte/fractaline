Fractaline — Support

Thanks for coloring with Fractaline: Infinite Coloring! Here are quick answers to common questions:

How do I restore my unlocked packs on another iPhone or iPad?

Tap Unlock (or Studio Pro) in the top-right corner of the Home screen and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the store sheet. Family Sharing is supported automatically.

How do I zoom, pan, rotate, or undo while coloring?

Use two fingers to pinch-to-zoom, pan around the canvas, or rotate your artwork. Tap with two fingers anytime for quick Undo, or tap the magnifying glass button in the top bar to reset zoom.

How do I adjust Music, Binaural Beats, Rain, ASMR, or Dark Mode?

Tap the waveform / settings button in the top bar (from Home or while coloring) to choose your App Theme (System, Light, or Dark), live studio backdrop, music mood, and individual volume sliders for Music, Binaural Beats, Soft Rain, and Pencil ASMR.
